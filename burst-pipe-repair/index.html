Burst pipe right now: the first five minutes

A burst pipe can release a lot of water in a short time. Your goal is to stop the flow, protect the people and electrical systems, and record the damage.

1

Close the main shutoff

Turn the main water valve clockwise until it stops. Common spots are near the water heater, in a garage or utility closet, or where the line enters the house.

2

Kill power to wet areas

If water is near outlets, a panel, or light fixtures, shut off those breakers only if you can reach the panel on dry ground. Otherwise stay out and tell the dispatcher.

3

Open the lowest faucet

With the main closed, open a tap on the lowest level and then the highest, so the pipes drain and pressure drops.

4

Photograph everything

Take photos and short video of the break, the water, and damaged belongings before you clean up. Your insurer will ask.

Do not try to patch a spraying pipe with tape or a clamp from a hardware store while the line is full. Once the water is off, call us and we will take it from there.

Why pipes crack, split, and burst

Most burst pipes started as something smaller. Corrosion thins the wall, a joint pulls apart, or a pressure spike from a faulty regulator stresses a weak spot. Ice can split a pipe from the inside.

A cracked or broken pipe may not spray at all. It may seep behind drywall for weeks. If you have a stain or a bill that does not add up, our leak detection page explains how we find hidden breaks before they become bursts.

Frozen pipes: thaw them safely and know when they have burst

If a faucet runs a trickle or nothing, and the pipe feels cold or frosty, the water inside may be frozen. Frozen pipes are not yet burst pipes, but ice can split the wall as it expands, and the leak shows only after the ice melts.

How to thaw a frozen pipe without making it worse

  • ✓Open the faucet served by the pipe so melting water and pressure have a place to go.
  • ✓Warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer or heating pad, starting near the faucet.
  • ✓Never use an open flame, a propane torch, or a heat gun. They can ignite framing or damage the pipe.
  • ✓If you cannot find the frozen section, or it is inside a wall, stop and call.
  • ✓Once flow returns, check the pipe and joints for drips.

Watch for a bulge, a hairline crack, or a hissing sound as the ice melts. If water appears, close the main and treat it as a burst. A pipe that froze once is a candidate for insulation or rerouting, and a pattern of splits may point toward whole house repiping.

Copper, supply, and drain pipe repairs

Copper pipe repair usually means cutting out the damaged section and joining new pipe with fittings. Pinholes along a copper line can come from corrosion inside the pipe. One pinhole may be a spot repair. Several in the same run suggest the pipe is wearing out.

Supply leaks carry pressure, so even a small crack wets a lot of material. A broken drain may show up only after a shower or a laundry load. Drain repairs under a house or yard may overlap with sewer line repair.
