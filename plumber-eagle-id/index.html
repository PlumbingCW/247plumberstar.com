Check your bill before you call anyone about the water

Eagle Water Company serves Eagle and is regulated by the Idaho Public Utilities Commission, but providers vary by address. Your bill tells you which one is yours.

Living near the river: flooding and sump pumps

Flooding along the Boise River has been a recurring issue in Eagle, even with Arrowrock Dam and later dams. If your home sits on low ground near the water, a wet crawlspace or basement is a real possibility in a wet season.

A working sump pump is the first defense, and a backup power plan is the second. If yours stops or gets overwhelmed, call us and see sump pump installation and repair. We cannot say what the ground is like under your lot, so ask us to check the pump, the discharge line, and the float before the wet season, not during it.

What to do before we arrive

Close the main valve

Turn it clockwise. If it is stuck, do not force it.

Switch off nearby power

Breakers for the water heater and wet rooms go off. Stay out of standing water.

Clear the floor

Move furniture, rugs, and boxes. A wet vac helps if the outlet is dry and far from the water.
