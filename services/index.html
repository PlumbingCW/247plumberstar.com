Plumbing Services in Star, ID

Start with the problem, not the trade name. Water on the floor, no hot water, and a drain that will not clear are different jobs, and each section below points to the page that explains how we handle it.

Our line is open 24/7 at (208) 679-8357. Emergencies get priority, and planned work is scheduled around you.

Water where it should not be

A pipe that has split or a hidden leak is the most urgent kind of call. For an active spray or flood, see burst pipe repair. If your water bill is climbing or you hear water running with everything off, leak detection finds the source without tearing up the whole house.

A leak between the street and your house is a different job. Our water service line repair page covers the pipe that feeds your home, and repiping is the answer when old pipes keep failing in one spot after another.

Drains and sewer lines

A slow sink and a backed-up main are not the same problem. Start with drain cleaning for clogs in sinks, tubs, and floor drains. When grease or roots keep bringing a clog back, hydro jetting scours the pipe wall with water pressure.

If the problem is the line that leaves your house, look at sewer line cleaning for blockages and sewer inspection when you want a camera to show what is inside. A cracked or collapsed pipe is covered under sewer line repair. Buying a house? A camera check before closing is a sensible use of the inspection page.

Not sure which problem you have?

Describe what you see and hear on the phone at (208) 679-8357. We will tell you which service fits.

Hot water

No hot water, a pilot that will not stay lit, or a tank that leaks all fall under heaters. Try water heater repair first, because many failures are a part and not the whole unit. When the tank itself is leaking or worn out, water heater replacement explains how to decide.

Planning a change on your own schedule? water heater installation covers a new tank, and tankless water heater explains what changes with an on-demand unit.

Toilets, faucets, and the kitchen

Everyday fixtures cause most of the small calls. A running or leaking toilet belongs on toilet repair. A dripping spout or weak flow is covered by faucet repair. If the disposal hums, jams, or leaks under the sink, see garbage disposal repair.

Planned work and bigger projects

Some jobs are not emergencies at all. plumbing installation covers new fixtures, new lines, and remodel work. If you have hard water and want to treat it, water softener and filtration walks through the options. Basement water is handled with sump pump installation and repair.

Businesses have their own needs, such as shared restrooms and larger drain lines, and commercial plumbing describes how we handle them.

All plumbing services

  • Drain Cleaning Cable and camera clog removal for kitchen sinks, showers, floor drains, and main lines, with 24/7 dispatch.
  • Water Heater Repair Repairs for leaking tanks, relief valve discharge, and no-hot-water failures on gas, electric, and hybrid water heaters.
  • Water Heater Replacement Replacement of failed gas and electric tanks, plus heat pump options, with venting, permit, and a line-by-line price.
  • Water Heater Installation Planned water heater installs for new builds, remodels, relocations, fuel conversions, and recirculation lines, with permits handled.
  • Tankless Water Heater Tankless flush and descale service, error code diagnosis, and sizing help for gas line and venting upgrades.
  • Hydro Jetting High-pressure water cleaning for grease, scale, silt, and roots, done after a camera check so weak pipe is not put at risk.
  • Leak Detection Acoustic, thermal, and pressure-test leak location for slab, wall, ceiling, crawlspace, and buried lines, with 24/7 dispatch.
  • Sewer Line Repair Camera-confirmed sewer repairs, from a single spot fix to full lateral replacement, with trenchless options when the pipe allows.
  • Sewer Camera Inspection A camera look inside the sewer line to find roots, cracks, bellies, and offsets, plus locating the pipe from above ground.

  • Faucet and Sink Repair Repair for dripping faucets, failed shutoff valves, supply lines, shower valves, tub spouts, and hose bibs, with 24/7 dispatch.

  • Commercial Plumbing After-hours leaks, commercial drains, floor drains, grease traps, and property manager support, with straight answers on scope.

  • Plumbing Installation Fixture installs, new and relocated lines, remodel rough-in, and new construction plumbing, with permits explained up front.
