What downtown Star is, and what it is not

A district, not a subdivision

Downtown Star is not a platted subdivision with a homeowners association. The city's own plans call it the Central Business District and describe it as the old town area, and they say its boundaries are poorly defined.

Because the edges are loose, this page uses streets as anchors. State Street, which is State Highway 44, runs through the middle. Main Street and Star Road cross it. The city's downtown plan splits the area into five districts: River, State Street Corridor, Star Road Corridor, Art, and Village.

The comprehensive plan also notes that some housing near the center is historic, and that early additions east of the original townsite were platted after the Boise Interurban line arrived. If you live downtown, your house may be one of the older ones in a young city. Our main Star plumbing page covers the rest of Star.

Older homes near Main Street

The city's history page says Star was incorporated in 1905 and that the Interurban electric line arrived in 1907. That tells you the town center is old by Star standards. It does not tell you what pipes are in any particular house.

Age alone does not prove a problem. A camera inspection shows the real condition of a drain or sewer line, and it is the right first step if backups keep coming back. See sewer camera inspection for how that works, and drain cleaning for clogs that clear.

If a water bill jumps and nothing looks wet, a hidden leak may be sending water into the ground. Water line repair covers the pipe between the meter and the house.

Shops, restaurants, and offices

Businesses downtown have different priorities than homeowners. A backed-up floor drain or a leak above a ceiling can close a counter for the day. Our commercial plumbing page explains what to expect.

Before you call about a business

  • ✓Know where the main water shutoff is and who in the building can reach it.
  • ✓Tell us if you lease. A landlord may own the pipe that is leaking.
  • ✓Note whether more than one fixture is affected, since that points to the main line.
  • ✓Stop using floor drains that are gurgling or overflowing.

Planned changes downtown

A local news report on the downtown plan says Star Road is set to widen to five lanes beginning in 2029, and that the city wants new development directed toward Main Street. We do not know how any specific project will affect a given building, so ask the city about timing. If new plumbing symptoms start around nearby construction, mention that when you call.
