A leaking water heater: what to do in the first ten minutes

A tank stays connected to a pressurized supply line that keeps refilling it, so a small leak can flood a garage or closet. Closing the supply is the most useful thing you can do before we arrive.

If you smell gas, do not touch switches or the heater. Leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first, then call us.

Shut it down in this order

  • ✓Turn off the power: flip the breaker for an electric unit, or set a gas unit's control to off or pilot.
  • ✓Close the cold water shutoff valve on the pipe feeding the top of the tank. Turn the handle until it stops.
  • ✓Open a hot faucet somewhere in the house to let pressure out and slow the leak.
  • ✓Move boxes, paint cans, and anything absorbent away from the puddle. If the floor is wet near outlets, stay out and leave the breaker alone.

Tank leak or fitting leak: why the source decides everything

Water under a heater does not always mean the tank has failed. A loose connector, a weeping drain valve, or a bad relief valve can all wet the floor while the tank is sound. Those are repairs. A tank rusted through is not.

Dry the area, wrap a paper towel around each fitting, and see which gets wet first. Water from the bottom of the jacket or the base seam means the steel tank is leaking.

Wet fitting on top

Supply and hot outlet connections can loosen or corrode. Tightening or replacing a connector or nipple is a normal repair.

Dripping drain valve or relief valve

Both are replaceable parts. A drain valve that weeps often just needs a new valve, and a discharging relief valve needs a diagnosis first.

Water from the tank body

Water from the jacket, the bottom, or the burner or element compartment means the tank itself has failed. We do not patch a tank. The answer is a replacement.

A heater on a garage slab can send water creeping to the slab edge near the door, well away from the tank. Trace the wet line back toward the heater, and tell the dispatcher what you found. Leaks inside walls or under floors are a job for leak detection.

Relief valve discharge: a safety device doing its job

The temperature and pressure relief valve is the last safety on a water heater. It opens when pressure or temperature climbs too high and sends hot water down a discharge pipe. A drip from that pipe means something pushed the tank past its limit, or the valve is worn.

Common causes are a failed thermostat that overheats the water, high incoming pressure, or a closed system with no room for thermal expansion. A valve dripping for months can also be fouled by mineral buildup.

Never block, cap, or plug the relief valve line

Do not put a cap, a plug, or a valve on the relief valve or its discharge pipe, even to stop a drip. A sealed relief line can let a tank build pressure until it fails violently. If the valve is discharging, shut off the heater's power or gas, then call us and let a plumber find the reason.

No hot water: working through the likely causes

The first question is the fuel, because gas and electric heaters fail in different ways. Check the simple things below, and call before you open any panel or burner cover.

Lukewarm water that runs out fast often means one failed element or heavy sediment. We sort these out by testing.

Repairing gas, electric, and hybrid water heaters

The three types share a tank and a relief valve, but different parts fail.

Gas water heater repair

A gas unit has a burner, a pilot or igniter, a flue, and a gas control valve. Most no-heat calls trace to ignition or a draft problem that trips the safety. We check venting and combustion air on every gas repair.

Electric water heater repair

An electric tank has two elements and two thermostats on a dedicated circuit. We test with a meter before replacing anything, because a tripped breaker can also point to a shorted element.

Hybrid and heat pump water heater repair

A hybrid unit adds a heat pump with a fan, compressor, and sensors, and many keep backup elements. Display codes, clogged filters, and condensate drains cause many calls. Sealed-system and warranty work may belong to the manufacturer's service network, and we will say so.

Repair or replace: a decision table

Manufacturers publish an expected service life for their tanks, and the label on yours shows the manufacture date. We do not guess your tank's remaining life. We look at the leak and the tank, and compare repair cost with a new heater.

What drives the cost of a water heater repair

We state the price before work starts, because the part and the access change the job. Relief valves and discharge pipes are safety items covered by the 2015 Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) that Idaho adopts as its State Plumbing Code.

  • The failed partA relief valve or an element is a different job from a gas control valve or a heat pump sensor board.
  • Fuel and unit typeHybrid and some high-efficiency units cost more to service than a basic electric or atmospheric gas tank.
  • Access and locationA heater in an open garage is faster to work on than one wedged in an attic or tight closet.
  • Supporting workCorroded shutoffs, a bad expansion tank, or an unsafe vent can add to the visit if they have to be fixed first.
  • TimingA night or weekend call-out can be priced differently from a scheduled weekday visit, and we say so up front.
