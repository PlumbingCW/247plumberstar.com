Plumbing Service Area: Star, Middleton, and Eagle

We send plumbers to Star, Middleton, and Eagle, Idaho. The same number, (208) 679-8357, reaches us in all three towns at any hour.

Below you will find the ZIP codes, what makes each town a little different to work in, and what to do if you live in a neighboring city.

☎ Call (208) 679-8357

Star, Idaho (ZIP 83669)

Star is home base. Our address is 10166 W State St, on State Highway 44. Most of the city sits in Ada County, with a small part in Canyon County, so ask which county your address falls in if you need a permit or a records question answered.

Star's population roughly doubled between 2010 and 2020, which means many homes are newer and many yards are still young. That affects drain and sewer work, and our drain cleaning and sewer line repair pages explain how.

Middleton (ZIP 83644)

Middleton is in Canyon County, about six miles west of Star on State Highway 44. It has its own City Hall, a Building Department, and utility billing for water, sewer, and trash. Our Middleton page lists what to do before we arrive and which services people ask for most.

Water on the floor in Middleton or Eagle?

Call (208) 679-8357 any hour. We cover the Highway 44 corridor and will confirm your address on the call.

Eagle (ZIP 83616)

Eagle is in Ada County, about seven road miles east of Star on State Highway 44. Water providers vary by address, so check your bill to see who serves you before you call about the supply side. Our Eagle page has the details, including emergency steps and nearby landmarks.

Why State Highway 44 matters to us

State Highway 44, also called State Street in Star, is the road that ties our three towns together. Middleton sits to the west, Eagle to the east, and our base is in the middle. When a call comes in, the dispatcher thinks in terms of this corridor, so a caller on Highway 44 is easy to place.

For you, the practical point is simple. Give the dispatcher your street, the nearest cross street, and the town name. Star, Middleton, and Eagle each have their own ZIP code, and the ZIP helps us confirm that the address in our system is the one you mean.

Utilities differ from one town to the next. Star homes are served by the Star Sewer and Water District in much of the city, Middleton runs its own utility billing, and Eagle water providers vary by address. When a problem might sit on the utility side of the line, we will tell you who to call. The contact page shows what to have ready.

Nearby cities people ask about

We get calls from people in Meridian, Nampa, and Caldwell. These cities do not have their own pages on this site, and we do not list them as standard service towns. If you live there, call (208) 679-8357 and ask. The dispatcher will tell you honestly whether we can reach your address.

What to have ready when you call from any of these towns

Dispatch goes faster when the plumber knows where you are and what you see. Our line is open 24/7, so there is no wrong hour to call, but a few details help the first conversation.

Details that help the dispatcher

  • ✓Your street address and the nearest cross street or subdivision, so we can confirm you are inside the area we cover.
  • ✓What the problem looks like: water on a floor, a drain that will not move, a tank that is cold, or a smell.
  • ✓Whether you already shut off the main water valve or the water heater, and whether it helped.
  • ✓A good call-back number and whether someone will be home to let a plumber in.
  • ✓Any photos you took, since they help the plumber bring the right parts.

We will confirm the price before work starts and tell you what we know about timing. We do not promise an arrival window, because traffic, distance and job length change through the day.
