Where Heron River sits in Star

A real estate profile places Heron River about a mile east of downtown Star, off State Highway 44, with the Boise River running along the community. Listing records for homes there show streets such as West Wildbranch Drive, South Rivermist Avenue, and South Barkvine Way. The city's comprehensive plan also names the Heron River Development when it describes a public road to be extended to Star Road.

Published boundaries for the neighborhood are unclear, so this page describes it by those anchor streets and does not draw a line. If you are not sure your address is inside Heron River, give your street when you call. The main Star plumbing page covers the rest of the city.

One listing profile describes about 575 homesites on lots from 0.12 to 0.44 acres, and the homes in its listings were built between 2013 and 2024. Those figures come from a real estate site and not from city records, so treat them as estimates.

Source type: Real estate listing profile, not an official count

What the community's features mean for plumbing

Community and listing pages describe ponds, walking paths, a club with a pool, gym, and tennis courts, more than 75 acres of protected open space, and pressurized irrigation. Only some of that touches the plumbing inside your house.

Pressurized irrigation

Pressurized irrigation waters landscaping and does not share pipes with your kitchen or bathrooms. If the leak is in a sprinkler line or a valve box, an irrigation contractor is usually the right call, and we will say so if that is what you describe.

Homes near ponds and the river

A home near water does not automatically have water problems. Still, damp floors, a musty smell, or water at a floor drain deserve a call, because the source could be a supply leak, a drain, or groundwater.

Homes built across about a decade

Listing records show Heron River homes built across roughly ten years. Water heaters, toilets, and faucets installed in different years sit at different points in their life, so age matters more than the neighborhood name.

Common areas

If the problem is in a shared area such as a path, a pond edge, or the club, contact your homeowners association instead. A plumber called to your house cannot work on property the association controls.

Before you call about a leak

  • ✓Shut off the fixture's own valve if you can reach it, or the main water valve if you cannot.
  • ✓Move rugs, boxes, and electronics away from the water.
  • ✓Turn off power at the breaker only if you can reach the panel without standing in water.
  • ✓Take photos of the damage for your records before you start cleaning.
  • ✓Stop using any drain that is backing up, and do not run the dishwasher or washer.

These steps are general safety advice for any house. They do not depend on where you live in Star.

Water, sewer, and permits

A public water system record for Star lists the Star Sewer and Water District as the provider, with groundwater as the source. Your bill will show who serves your address, and that is the party to ask about service lines and meters.

The Idaho Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses says a permit is required when plumbing work is performed, and that unlicensed plumbing work can bring civil penalties. You can check a plumber's license by calling DOPL at 208-334-3233.
