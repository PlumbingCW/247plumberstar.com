Plumbing Guides for Star Homeowners

These guides answer the questions Star homeowners ask when something in the house stops working. Each one is written in plain language, with honest notes on what you can do yourself and when to stop.

Some topics are simple repairs, like swapping a faucet cartridge. Others, such as gas water heaters and sewer problems, involve safety or costly damage, and the guide will say so early.

☎ Call (208) 679-8357

What you will find here

We cover water heaters, drains, sewer lines, toilets, faucets, sump pumps, and leak hunting. Guides explain the method, list the steps, and point out the mistakes that turn a small repair into a bigger one.

We do not print prices in these guides. Costs depend on the house, the access, and the parts, so the cost guides explain what drives a quote and what to ask for in writing.

How to use them

  • Read the first paragraph. It answers the main question quickly.
  • Check the safety notes before you pick up a tool. Shut off water, power, or gas as the guide says.
  • Stop when the guide says to call a plumber. Some repairs are not worth the risk.
  • Call (208) 679-8357 if you are unsure. A short call can save an afternoon.

When reading is not enough

A guide cannot see inside your wall. If water is spreading, a drain is backing up, or you smell gas, put the guide down. For gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911. For water and drains, call our 24/7 line and we will talk you through the first steps.

Our services page sorts every job we handle by problem, which can help when you are not sure what to search for.
