Not every plumbing problem needs a midnight call. Some do, because the damage grows by the hour. If you are unsure, call and describe it, and the dispatcher will say whether it can wait.

How we price a plumbing job

A plumber looks first, then tells you the price, and work begins only after you say yes. These are the things that move it.

  • What is actually wrongA worn washer and a split pipe behind a wall are very different jobs, even when both show up as a drip.
  • How hard it is to reachOpen basement pipe is quick. Pipe inside a finished wall or under a slab takes more time and more repair work afterward.
  • Parts and materialsFixture, valve, and pipe choices change the total. We explain the options so you choose what fits.
  • Permits and inspectionsSome work needs a permit. Idaho DOPL requires one for plumbing work, and we tell you up front if your job is covered.
  • Time of dayA night or weekend call-out can be priced differently from a weekday visit, and we say so before you agree.
