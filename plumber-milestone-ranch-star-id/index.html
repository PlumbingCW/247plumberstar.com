Warranty first, then a plumber

New homes often come with a builder warranty that covers certain plumbing items for a set period. The terms differ by builder and by contract, and we have not seen yours. Reading it first can save you a service charge.

1

Read the warranty

Look for the plumbing items it covers, the time limits, and how to submit a claim.

2

Stop any damage

Shut off the fixture valve or the main valve. Warranty claims do not require you to let water keep running.

3

Call the builder or call us

If the warranty applies, file the claim. If it does not, or the builder cannot come soon, call and we will talk through the options.

Ask before you pay for a repair

Some warranties are voided by outside repairs. If you are unsure, ask the builder in writing before anyone opens a wall or replaces a part.
