Pinewood Lakes and The Reserve, west of downtown Star

Plumber in Pinewood Lakes, Star, ID

Pinewood Lakes sits on the west side of Star, off State Street, with a gated section called The Reserve at Pinewood Lakes. If you have a leak, a clogged drain, or a water heater that has stopped working, call (208) 679-8357 and tell us what you are seeing.

The community mixes single-family homes, patio homes, villas, and custom homes, and listing records show a wider range of build years than most newer Star subdivisions. That range shapes the plumbing questions below.

The city gives a useful anchor for the location. A City of Star staff report for the Cranefield Phase 3 plat, at 12667 West State Street, says Pinewood Lakes is adjacent to the east and that the new subdivision's east access is at West Bridger Bay Drive inside Pinewood Lakes.

That puts the community on the south side of State Street, west of downtown. Exact boundaries are not published in the sources we found, so if you are unsure whether your address is in Pinewood Lakes, give your street when you call. Our main Star plumbing page covers the rest of the city.

What older homes mean for your fixtures

Listing records show Pinewood Lakes homes built as early as 2005, and the listings we reviewed run through 2019. Star as a whole is young, with a median build year of 2018 on one housing site, so a home from the mid 2000s is among the older ones in the city. That does not mean anything is failing. It means parts have had time to wear.

Patio homes, villas, and The Reserve

Patio homes and villas

Smaller homes still have a water heater, a main shutoff, and drains that can back up. Learn where your main valve is, because a quick shutoff does the most to limit damage. If a neighbor shares a wall with you, tell them when you have a leak.

The gated Reserve

The listing profile describes The Reserve as a gated section with larger, waterfront homesites meant for custom homes. Larger custom homes tend to have more bathrooms and more fixtures, which gives a slow leak more places to hide. If a water bill jumps and you cannot see why, leak detection is the next step. Tell us when you call that the road is gated so access is not a surprise.

Water, sewer, and the HOA

A public water system record for Star lists the Star Sewer and Water District, which draws from groundwater. Check your bill to see who serves your address. Pressurized irrigation is a separate system from your household water, so ask your homeowners association or an irrigation contractor about sprinklers.

Common areas such as the park, the pool, and the lakes belong to the association. A plumber called to your home cannot work on those.

Questions from Pinewood Lakes homeowners
