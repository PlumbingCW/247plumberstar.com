The first ten minutes of a Middleton water emergency

Most of the damage in a plumbing emergency happens before the plumber arrives. Water keeps running and a small leak becomes a ruined floor.

If you are not sure which valve controls the house, call us and we will describe where to look.

Shut off the main valve

Turn the house main clockwise until it stops. If it will not move, leave it and tell the dispatcher.

Kill power near the water

Switch off breakers for wet rooms, the water heater, and any outlet near standing water.

Clear the area

Lift rugs, electronics, and boxes off the floor. Pull the kids and pets out of the wet room.

Call the 24/7 line

Dial (208) 679-8357. Tell us the street, the nearest cross street to Highway 44, and where the water is coming from.

Smell gas? Do not call us first.

Leave the building, then call the gas utility or 911 from outside. Call a plumber only after the gas company says the area is safe.

City water, a city bill, and the leak you cannot see

The City of Middleton draws its drinking water from 7 wells spread across town, according to its 2022 consumer confidence report. It bills water, sewer, and trash together through City Hall at 1103 West Main Street, (208) 585-3133.

That billing setup gives you a simple test. If your bill climbs and nothing in the house changed, shut off every fixture and watch the meter. A moving dial means water is escaping somewhere on your side, and that is a leak detection job. If the leak is between the meter and the house, our water service line repair page explains the fix.

Source type: City of Middleton consumer confidence report and city website

For trouble in the street, such as a main break or low pressure on your whole block, the city runs an after-hours public works line at (208) 921-0029. Call us when the problem is on the house side.

Water heaters, drains, and what Middleton calls us about

Hot water trouble leads the list, then drains, then hidden leaks. A tank that drips from the bottom seam is finished, and a tank that only lacks hot water often needs a part. Our water heater repair page helps you tell the two apart, and water heater replacement covers what happens when it is time for a new one.

Plumbing work in Idaho needs a state permit, and a city building permit may not stand in for it. Ask the Middleton Building Department what it wants on your project, and ask any plumber, us included, how the state permit and inspections will be handled.

Tank leaking

Shut the cold supply to the tank, switch off its power or gas control, and call. Do not wait for it to dry up.

Drain backing up

Stop running water. If the lowest drain backs up first, the main line is likely blocked. See drain cleaning.

Pipe burst

Main off, breaker off, towels down, then call.

An old town does not tell you what is under your lot

Middleton is the oldest settlement in Canyon County, with land first parceled out in 1863 and a grist mill running by 1871. A flood in 1872 cut a new river channel and pushed settlement away from the original site after 1880.

That history says very little about the pipes in your house. A sewer camera inspection shows the real condition of a house line in minutes, so you can decide whether to repair, clean, or leave it alone.
